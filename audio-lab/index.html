What this prototype measures

✓ Spectral energy and timbre-band changes
✓ Chroma / harmonic-colour shifts
✓ Loudness and dynamic changes
✓ Onset activity and approximate tempo
✓ Self-similarity novelty / candidate boundaries
✓ Playback-clock cue activation

What it does not claim

This is DSP-based structural analysis, not yet MOSS-Music inference. It does not identify a named soloist or instrument entrance with semantic certainty, perform score alignment, or prove a formal section label. Detected changes are candidates for review.
