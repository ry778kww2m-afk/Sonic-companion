# Product direction: Music Immersion

## Naming hypothesis

“Music Immersion” is a strong descriptor for the experience because it expresses active, sustained discovery rather than a static guide. Keep **Sonic Companion** as the working app name for now. Evaluate “Music Immersion” as either a product name or a feature name through user testing before rebranding.

Possible positioning: **Music Immersion — hear more in the music you already love.** This is a concept line, not approved brand copy.

## Personal interpretation hypothesis

A future opt-in feature could let a listener add personal observations to an exact track or recording:

- What they notice (instrument, motif, groove, harmony, emotional turn).
- Their own interpretation or association.
- What they want the next listening pass to help them notice.

The engine could use those notes to ask better follow-up questions and vary the listening lens on a later replay. It must not silently convert a subjective note into an objective claim. Keep personal notes separate from source-backed composition facts, make the feature opt-in, make notes editable/deletable, and prefer local-first storage unless the user explicitly chooses sync.

Validation before building: test whether listeners actually enter notes, whether the notes improve repeat listening, and whether this increases session depth without making listening feel like homework.
