BRIDGE BEHAVIOUR
- Audio Lab writes analysis records to sonic.audioAnalysis.registry.v1.
- Main app accepts a record only for an exact Spotify track ID match and duration within +/-3 seconds.
- Candidate markers are additive; authored guide cues are preserved.
- Audio measurements describe signal changes only. They do not identify instruments, solos, performers, or musical events.
- Recording confirmation is manual; the app does not acoustically fingerprint the Spotify stream.
- Spotify playback controls and OAuth implementation are not intentionally changed.
